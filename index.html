Widgets Plus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Widgets Plus (Android package com.widgetsplus.app, iOS bundle com.themejunky.widgetsplus) is published by ThemeJunky (data controller; contact below). It offers home-screen and lock-screen widgets, icon sets and wallpapers with themes. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. See also the Terms of Use.

Your widgets stay on your device

Widget settings (type, size, theme), countdown titles and dates, quotes, the city or coordinates you choose for weather, and your unlocked themes are stored on the device only (on iOS in the app group shared with the widget extension). Photos you pick for the photo widget are read through the system photo picker, resized on the device and never uploaded. Wallpapers are saved to your device with your permission. App backup is disabled on Android.

Weather

If you use a weather widget, the app sends the city name or the approximate coordinates you chose to the weather service over HTTPS to get the forecast. Approximate location is read only after you tap the location button in the weather settings and only while you configure it; no background location is used. You can always type a city instead. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob and its mediation partners (for example Liftoff Monetize and, on Android, Meta Audience Network). Ad networks may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions, as described in the Google advertising policy and the partners' policies. Where required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in the app settings (Privacy options). On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking. Ads are never shown inside widgets. Premium removes ads.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. On iOS this collection is off until you opt in, and you can withdraw in Settings → Privacy. The data is limited to anonymous usage events (for example: setup completed, widget added, theme applied), device model, OS version, app version, country and language, and crash stack traces. Analytics is configured without the advertising ID. No widget content, photo, title, quote or coordinates is ever included. We do not sell this data. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security. Analytics data is retained for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can show reminders about new themes and offers. Your device's push token is registered with our server (admin.chrisbarin.com) together with the app version, language and time zone, and is deleted when it becomes invalid or when you uninstall. You can turn these notifications off in the app settings at any time.

Support

If you contact us from the app, your message, the e-mail you type and basic device information (model, OS version, app version, language) are sent to our support service so we can reply by e-mail.

Purchases

Widgets Plus Premium is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; purchase status is verified through the store on your device.

Permissions (Android)

Photos through the system picker: photo widget. Approximate location (optional): weather. Set wallpaper: apply a wallpaper you chose. Notifications: theme updates and reminders. Internet and network state: weather, ads, Firebase, billing.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
